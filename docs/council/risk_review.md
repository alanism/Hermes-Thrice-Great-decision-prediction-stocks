## Waiver Protocol

(Willison Q-W7, adopted as the canonical rule; Carlini's cost-to-attack release valve and Lopopolo's human-owned gates are compatible with it.)

- Any gate consciously waived must be recorded in the build ledger with **governor initials and one sentence of accepted risk**.
- **Silent skipping is not a waiver** — it is the normalization of deviance this council exists to prevent.
- A recorded waiver is legitimate; unrecorded gate-skips fail the phase.
- Waivers apply to gates the governor owns; hard stops (security/privacy/legal/production/credentials/scope) are never waivable by the council itself — they escalate to the human.
- Waived gates remain in the register with status `WAIVED(<date>, <initials>)`; MR-16/MR-17 (egress, injection-refusal) may be waived only under an explicit trusted-corpus governor ruling (Decisions 8/9), never by silence.
