# Stock Exposure Decision Lab

The primary product promise is:

> A local decision lab for stock exposure, upside convexity, Kelly sizing, and margin safety.

The default workflow is:

1. Enter a concentrated-position thesis and explicit assumptions.
2. Calculate the broader return and risk stack already supported by the lab.
3. Examine full, confidence-adjusted, fractional, and user-capped Kelly references.
4. Compare bear, base, bull, and optional tail-upside scenarios for payoff asymmetry.
5. Stress manual margin inputs for threshold and liquidation-buffer behavior.
6. Review unavailable metrics and warnings.
7. Keep any selected action separate from explicit human authorization and local ledger recording.

Analysis and comparison output is organized into decision summary, Kelly sizing, convexity/upside asymmetry, margin safety, scenario analysis, unavailable metrics, warnings, and provenance/hashes. Comparison remains unranked and does not choose an alternative.

The broader engine remains intact: absolute return, ROI, CAGR, expected value and return, volatility, downside deviation, drawdown, tail risk, correlation, risk-adjusted ratios, scenario analysis, seeded Monte Carlo, authorization validation, and atomic local records.
